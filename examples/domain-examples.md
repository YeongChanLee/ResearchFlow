# Domain-neutral planning examples

These are **illustrative prompts**, not completed or approved studies.

## Qualitative inquiry
"Use ResearchFlow to plan interviews about how junior scientists decide when to rely
on AI. Discuss recruitment/access, consent/confidentiality, researcher reflexivity,
analysis tradition and trustworthiness. Do not assume a directional hypothesis,
statistical significance test, or inter-rater metric fits the selected methodology."

## Theoretical research
"Use ResearchFlow to specify a theoretical question about a class of estimators.
Clarify definitions and assumptions, reconstruct relevant proof strategies from
accessible papers, plan lemmas and counterexample checks, and ask for confirmation.
Do not invent a dataset or require model-training metrics. Do not claim to have proved
anything as part of planning."

## Laboratory study
"Use ResearchFlow to plan a perturbation experiment. Co-design the question, controls,
experimental units, biological/technical replication, materials, measurement, batch
considerations and validation. State which materials are planned versus actually
available. Stop at the approved specification; do not execute an experiment."

## Computational study
"Use ResearchFlow to design an evaluation of a predictive model under distribution
shift. Review prior evaluation workflows, available data and licenses, split units,
leakage prevention, baselines and uncertainty. Do not train models or run analyses."
