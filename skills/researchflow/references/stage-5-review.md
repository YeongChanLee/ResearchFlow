# Stage 5 — Specification Review & Baseline Approval

## Purpose
Review the integrated living specification and record a scoped, versioned baseline.
A complete document is not necessarily a scientifically sound or executable design.

## Review in distinct passes
1. **Completeness:** are required decisions specified, with justified N/A where needed?
2. **Evidence:** can load-bearing claims be traced to inspected sources; are source
   access limitations and uncertain statements represented accurately?
3. **Consistency:** do question, resources, definitions, procedures and evaluation
   agree across the document? Do changes propagate to dependent sections?
4. **Feasibility:** distinguish an approved plan from verified access/availability.
   Are unresolved acquisition conditions realistic and explicitly acknowledged?
5. **Scientific critique:** alternatives, plausible failure, bias, inference limits,
   robustness, negative findings and domain-specific concerns.
6. **Governance:** explicit scoped confirmations, unresolved issues, change records,
   exposure to results, reproducibility and handoff boundaries.

Record findings as **blocking**, **conditional**, or **advisory** with location,
reason, proposed action and resolution. Do not silently rewrite confirmed decisions
while reviewing. A separate agent/reviewer can help only if actually available;
record who reviewed, and never label self-review an independent expert review.

## Decision at the gate
- **Draft / awaiting confirmation:** missing decisions or explicit approvals.
- **Blocked:** an essential contradiction or unresolved baseline-level issue.
- **Approved, conditional:** the design is approved, but listed acquisition/execution
  prerequisites remain. This is not readiness to run the study.
- **Approved for handoff:** design checks and known prerequisites are satisfied;
  still no automatic authorization to collect data or execute research.

Present the exact version/digest, relevant changes, review findings and conditions
for explicit researcher approval. With the helper, all five stage confirmations
must be current; the final receipt must acknowledge each remaining condition ID.
The helper's readiness output is bookkeeping, not scientific certification.

## Save and hand off
Snapshot the current specification and supporting records without overwriting any
old baseline. Record version, time, content digests, approval provenance, prerequisites,
and limitations. Keep the living draft separate. Never claim preregistration without
a real registry record. After a substantive amendment, re-review affected stages
and create a new baseline with renewed Stage 5 approval.
