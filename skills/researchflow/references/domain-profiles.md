# Domain profiles — select, do not force

These are planning prompts, not exhaustive professional guidelines. Retrieve current
primary methodological guidance when a consequential domain-specific decision needs
it. A study can combine profiles. Document why a field is not applicable.

## Computational / machine learning
Task and unit of prediction; input/output; data provenance and licensing; target and
label definitions; train/validation/test independence; grouping/time/site leakage;
preprocessing fit boundaries; baselines and compute comparability; model selection;
external/shift/subgroup evaluation; uncertainty, reproducibility and failure cases.

## Laboratory / experimental
Experimental unit; intervention/perturbation; controls; materials and provenance;
measurement and instrument calibration; biological versus technical replication;
randomization/blinding when appropriate; batch effects; sample-size rationale;
safety/ethics requirements; reproducibility; exclusion, stopping and failure rules.

## Observational / causal
Target population and inference; time zero and eligibility; exposure/comparison and
outcomes; estimand where applicable; operational definitions; confounding, selection,
missingness, time-related biases; identification assumptions; estimation, diagnostics,
sensitivity and interpretation. Reporting checklists do not certify design validity.

## Qualitative / interpretive
Question and epistemic stance; sampling/access; interview/observation/text sources;
consent/confidentiality; researcher position/reflexivity; analytic approach; coding and
interpretation audit; divergent cases; triangulation or other trustworthiness criteria
appropriate to the chosen tradition. Do not require quantitative hypothesis testing
or intercoder reliability when incompatible with the selected methodology.

## Theoretical / mathematical
Problem, definitions, axioms and assumptions; relation to existing results; intended
claim and domain; proof strategy and lemmas; counterexamples/edge cases; independent
checking or formal verification plans where appropriate; dependence on computational
claims. Proofs, simulations and automated theorem execution are subsequent work.

## Simulation / engineering design
System boundary; model assumptions; input provenance; parameter ranges; numerical
and structural verification; calibration versus validation; reference systems;
uncertainty/sensitivity; compute constraints; applicability limits and comparison.
Only design the simulation or prototype; do not execute it here.

## Humanities / archival / evidence synthesis
Question and interpretive frame; corpus/archives and selection rationale; provenance,
authenticity, access/language limitations; interpretive comparisons and contradictory
readings; researcher stance; traceable quotations and translations; synthesis method.
For systematic/scoping reviews, specify their actual search and selection protocol;
not every literature-supported project is such a review.

## Mixed methods
Specify why integration is needed, sequence/priority of components, shared concepts,
linkage/triangulation, disagreement handling and joint interpretation. Use applicable
profiles without pretending that the components have identical evidence standards.
