# Stage 3 — Data & Resource Co-design

## Purpose
Translate the question into the data, observations, materials, literature, assumptions,
and resources it actually requires. Do not limit resources to tabular variables.

## Procedure
1. Map each research objective to necessary inputs. Explain why each is required or
   optional. Include units, resolution, timing, linkage, provenance, and quality needs.
2. Separate existing resources from planned collection/generation. Existing resources
   need evidence of actual access and relevant contents; planned resources need an
   acquisition/sampling/measurement plan and contingencies.
3. Inspect available metadata, data dictionaries, schemas, official access conditions,
   or researcher reports. Do not process raw research data under this skill.
4. Discuss operational definitions and measurement validity with the researcher.
   Distinguish the concept of interest from its observable proxy. Alternatives may
   change the question; do not call them equivalent without justification.
5. Review permissions, ethical review, consent, privacy, copyright, budget, expertise,
   instruments, compute, and time where relevant. Identify approvals required without
   providing a legal/ethical clearance or implying an IRB approval exists.
6. Record evidence for availability and responsible parties/milestones where supplied.
   Do not invent confirmed sample sizes or assume separate datasets can be linked.
7. Compare feasible alternatives and seek confirmation of the resource/acquisition plan.

## Availability vocabulary (independent of decision approval)
- `unknown`: not verified.
- `catalogue_only`: a record exists; usable access/content not established.
- `access_pending`: access requested or awaiting a prerequisite.
- `planned_collection`: data/materials will be collected/generated outside this skill.
- `verified_usable`: evidence supports access plus the needed content/linkage/fitness.
- `unavailable`: a needed resource cannot currently be obtained/used as planned.

Use verification dates and evidence locators. A researcher's report can be evidence
but label it as reported, not independently audited. Availability can later deteriorate.

## Gate
Confirm definitions, acquisition plans and assumptions. A data-collection plan may be
approved before collection, subject to stated conditions. Critical unavailable inputs
require a change request, a feasible redesign, or a blocked status—not silent deletion
or substitution. For theoretical/interpretive work, document applicable assumptions,
archives, texts or materials; use justified N/A rather than inventing variables.
